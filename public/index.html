About Me

I am a Data Scientist at C6 Bank and a Computer Science Researcher (MSc, Federal University of Viçosa – UFV), with expertise spanning Financial Data Science, GeoAI, Graph Machine Learning, and Spatial Optimization.

My journey in Data Science began in 2019 at NESPeD-Lab, where I investigated large-scale geospatial data enrichment and authored MoreData, an open-source framework integrating OpenStreetMap and GeoPandas to semantically enrich mobility and urban datasets. In 2021, I was selected for Google Summer of Code (GSoC) with PySAL, implementing classic facility location and coverage optimization models (LSCP, MCLP, P-Center, and P-Median), and subsequently joined the core development team of the spopt library.

During my Master's degree, I focused on structural and positional graph neural networks for urban region representation learning—work recognized with the 3rd Place National Award at the Brazilian Computer Society's Thesis and Dissertation Contest (CTD-SBC 2026). Currently, as a Financial Data Scientist at C6 Bank, I apply machine learning, quantitative modeling, and scalable data pipelines to solve high-impact problems in the financial sector, while continuing research on graph learning, human mobility, and spatial decision-making.
