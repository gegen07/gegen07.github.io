  <h2>About Me</h2>
  <p>I am a <strong>Data Scientist at <a href="https://www.c6bank.com.br/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">C6 Bank</a></strong> and a <strong>Computer Science Researcher</strong> (MSc, Federal University of Viçosa – UFV), with expertise spanning <strong>Financial Data Science</strong>, <strong>GeoAI</strong>, <strong>Graph Machine Learning</strong>, and <strong>Spatial Optimization</strong>.</p>
  <p>My journey in Data Science began in 2019 at <a href="https://nesped.caf.ufv.br/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NESPeD-Lab</a>, where I investigated large-scale geospatial data enrichment and authored <a href="https://github.com/NESPEDUFV/more-data" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MoreData</a>, an open-source framework integrating OpenStreetMap and GeoPandas to semantically enrich mobility and urban datasets. In 2021, I was selected for <a href="https://gist.github.com/gegen07/576dcdfad046cc91246bdb3ea3a47ce1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Summer of Code (GSoC)</a> with <a href="https://pysal.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PySAL</a>, implementing classic facility location and coverage optimization models (LSCP, MCLP, P-Center, and P-Median), and subsequently joined the core development team of the <a href="https://github.com/pysal/spopt" target="_blank" rel="noopener">spopt</a> library.</p>
  <p>During my Master's degree, I focused on structural and positional graph neural networks for urban region representation learning—work recognized with the <strong>3rd Place National Award</strong> at the Brazilian Computer Society's Thesis and Dissertation Contest (CTD-SBC 2026). Currently, as a Financial Data Scientist at <strong>C6 Bank</strong>, I apply machine learning, quantitative modeling, and scalable data pipelines to solve high-impact problems in the financial sector, while continuing research on graph learning, human mobility, and spatial decision-making.</p>
